The A/C rescue crew

Blowing warm in the Tampa Bay heat? Bring it straight to us.

Riverview summers are no joke. A car with a dead A/C goes from "minor annoyance" to "cannot drive it" in an afternoon. Finding the leak fast is what we do — and we get you back to that first blast of cold air the same day whenever we can.

  • Fast leak diagnosis. We track down where the cold is escaping instead of just topping off refrigerant and sending you back into the heat.
  • Same-day when we can. Most A/C jobs are diagnosed and cooling again before the day is out.
  • Extended warranties handled. Covered by a plan like Endurance? We work with the warranty company and sort the paperwork so you don't have to.
